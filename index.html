01 — About

A home for ideas worth developing.

Hukm Qalbi is a creative organization. We take ideas from their earliest form to their finished expression — across design, technology, publishing, and media.

Founded in February 2024 by Saalim Zayed, Hukm Qalbi exists to create, develop, and amplify ideas.

We work as a single integrated practice — not a collection of separate services. Every project is treated as part of a longer story.

Our work is grounded in the belief that meaningful work begins with meaningful intention.
