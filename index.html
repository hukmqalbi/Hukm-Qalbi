01 — Philosophy

More than creativity. A purpose behind every idea.

Hukm Qalbi is a creative organization built around the belief that meaningful work begins with meaningful intention.

"Ideas begin as thoughts. We give them direction, form, and a place in the world."

Founded in February 2024 by Saalim Zayed, Hukm Qalbi exists to create, develop, and amplify ideas — taking them from their first form to their finished expression.

We work across design, technology, publishing, and media, treating every discipline as part of a single continuous practice. The result is work that feels considered rather than assembled.

We build for those who care about craft, clarity, and lasting value. Not noise. Not trends. Work that holds up.

04 — Publishing

Ideas that take the form of books.

The Long Game is the first book published under Hukm Qalbi — a short, deliberate work on patience, meaningful ambition, and the discipline of building something worth finishing.

The book is accompanied by a digital companion site and a reading experience built in-house. It reflects the same care in print that we bring to our design and product work.

06 — Founder

Saalim Zayed

Founder of Hukm Qalbi

Saalim Zayed founded Hukm Qalbi as a home for ideas that deserve to exist — spanning entrepreneurship, creativity, digital products, writing, brand building, and purpose-driven projects.

His work is grounded in the belief that clarity of intent is the difference between work that lasts and work that fades — and that the most meaningful projects are built slowly, with care.
